Who we are

A hub built for people who actually want to build

Grey Academy was created to close the gap between classroom theory and what technology teams need on day one.

Our story

We started with a small room, a handful of laptops and a stubborn belief: talent is everywhere, structured training is not. Today Grey Academy runs five specialist tracks, each with a tutor who knows every student by name and a portal where materials, videos and tutorials live in one place.

How we teach

Every cohort follows the same rhythm: a weekly lesson, a hands-on project, a tutor review, and a peer session. By the time you graduate, you've shipped real work — not just watched someone else build.
